The basics
Price is $29, then tax is added?

Yes, that's the usual set-up. The plan shows $29. At checkout Stripe reads the buyer's billing country and card and adds that country's VAT/GST/sales tax. A German pays $34.51; a Floridian pays $29.

Does ABC get $29 + tax?

It receives it, but the tax part isn't ABC's money. It holds it for a few weeks, then pays it to that country's tax office. It keeps $29 minus Stripe fees.

What is a "return"?

A tax return is a periodic report plus payment: "This quarter we collected $X of German VAT; here it is." It's filed monthly, quarterly or yearly depending on the country.

What tax does ABC itself pay?

Profit tax in its home country (Switzerland here, roughly 12–21% depending on canton), only on profit after costs. The VAT/GST collected is passed on, not paid from ABC's own pocket.

Does Stripe handle everything?

Stripe Tax calculates, shows and collects the tax. ABC still registers, files returns and pays, unless it buys Stripe's filing service or uses a merchant of record that takes it all over.

Stripe's fee isn't the same for every payment

It depends on the country of the Stripe account (Switzerland in this example), where the subscriber's card is from, and whether currency is converted. Fees are a percentage of the total including tax.

Avoid the 2% conversion

  • Add USD and EUR bank accounts to Stripe so those currencies are paid out without converting.
  • Or price in each customer's local currency.
  • Try the "Payout in the same currency" toggle above to see the difference.

Negotiate at volume

  • Stripe offers custom pricing (volume discounts, interchange-plus) once monthly volume is large.
  • Worth asking once the subscriber base grows.

Encourage annual plans

  • One charge a year instead of twelve: the fixed CHF 0.30 is paid once.
  • Fewer failed renewals and fewer disputes.
  • Try "Annual" above and compare yearly fees.
Who does what

Stripe's job vs ABC's job

Stripe Tax does automatically

  • Reads billing address and card country to find the customer's location
  • Picks the right rate (country, US state and city, Canadian province)
  • Adds the tax on the checkout page and on the invoice
  • Validates business tax IDs and applies reverse charge (0%)
  • Collects the full amount, including tax, from the card
  • Produces reports: "tax collected per country this quarter"
  • Warns when sales approach a country's or US state's threshold

ABC must still do

  • Register in each country once required and enter the registration in Stripe
  • File the returns on time, monthly or quarterly
  • Pay the collected tax from its bank account to each tax office
  • Keep location evidence and records (10 years in the EU)
  • Decide pricing: tax on top, or included
  • Pay profit tax at home on its own profit
  • Stripe can file returns in many countries as a paid add-on (Tax Complete plans), or an accountant does it.

Or: a merchant of record does it all

  • Stripe Managed Payments or Paddle legally sells the plan
  • They register, collect, file and pay tax in every country
  • ABC gets one payout and only handles its own profit tax
  • Costs more: Managed Payments is about 3.5% extra per sale
  • Their name appears on the invoice as the seller
